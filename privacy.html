Privacy policy: Infusion Calc Pro

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Infusion Calc Pro does not collect any information about you or your patients.

The app has no account and does not connect to the internet. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. Nothing you type is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your device: your pinned drugs, the drugs and dilutions you save, any dose ranges you edit, and your settings such as pounds or kilograms. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export your dilutions to a file and share that file yourself.

The patient weight you type is kept in memory only. It is cleared after 60 minutes and when the app closes. No patient names or records are stored anywhere in the app.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
