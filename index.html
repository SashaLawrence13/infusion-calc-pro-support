Support: Infusion Calc Pro

Need help or found a number that looks wrong? Write to sashalawrence100@yahoo.com. Please include the drug, the dilution, the weight and the dose you entered, and the rate you expected. Reports of wrong numbers are fixed first.

Common questions

Where are my saved dilutions? Open a drug, tap the sliders button, and look under My unit's dilutions. They stay on your device and are never removed by an update.

How do I share my unit's dilutions with a colleague? Settings, then Export to a file. Send the file. Your colleague opens Settings, then Import from a file. Importing only adds. It never replaces what they already have.

Does the app need internet? No. It works with no signal.

Does the app store patient information? No. The weight you type is kept in memory for 60 minutes and then cleared.

The app is a calculation aid. Always check every result against the order, the pump library and your local protocol.
